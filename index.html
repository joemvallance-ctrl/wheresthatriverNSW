River lines from Natural Earth (1:10m), clipped to this sheet. They are generalised, so small meanders are smoothed out. Your score is the average gap between your line and the real course, measured both ways, so leaving a stretch out costs as much as overshooting.

How to play

  1. Read the river's name. The map has no labels, just the coast, lakes and state borders.
  2. Press and drag to trace the whole river in one stroke. Start from either end.
  3. Let go. The real river draws over your line, and the number shows how far apart they were on average. Lower is better.
